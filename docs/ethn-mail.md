# ETHN booking mail

New online submissions and staff-created bookings also queue a separate alert for each enabled ETHN owner. Delivery resolves the owner's current confirmed sign-in email from ETHN Auth and skips disabled, deactivated, deleted or email-less accounts. The alert includes the court schedule and an authenticated admin link, not customer contact details, payment proof or the private tracking link. Staff-created bookings still notify owners when no guest email was entered.

ETHN sends status updates for payment proof received, confirmation, rejection, cancellation and recorded refund. Every booking email, including owner alerts, has branded HTML and a plain-text alternative. Confirmations include an invoice with the booking date, original court ranges, saved slot intervals and hourly rates, line amounts, payment method when available, and paid total. Customer updates include the venue address and contact link; confirmations include directions. Online updates use the existing private tracking link or code-and-email lookup. Rejections lead with venue contact and keep tracking available as a secondary link. Staff-booked guest confirmations lead with directions, and other staff-booked guest updates lead with venue contact; neither includes private tracking. No email is sent to staff-booked guests without an address, and archiving never sends an update.

Booking emails embed the ETHN logo. Online payment-proof receipts and confirmations with a private tracking link also embed a tracking QR, not a check-in pass. The button and booking code still work without images. Receipts and confirmations show saved slot counts, durations, per-slot prices (marked approximate when rounding applies), line subtotals, and the booking or paid total. Confirmations include one optional Google Calendar event spanning the first start to the last end. The event description lists actual court ranges so gaps are clear; no private booking link or payment information is sent to Google.

Cancellation does not record a refund. A refund notice reports staff's record of an external refund, not an in-app transfer. Cancellation/refund staff notes, payment references and payment proofs are not included. No historical notifications are backfilled.

Apply `db/ethn/0006_outbox_access.sql` after migrations 0001–0005. Mail is disabled by default. To opt in, set `ETHN_MAIL_ENABLED=1`, `ETHN_MAIL_FROM` to the ETHN sender email, and `ETHN_APP_URL` to the HTTPS ETHN origin. Configure the existing server-only SMTP or Gmail transport separately; do not use legacy branding settings as ETHN sender or origin.

For `npm run dev` with Mailpit, use `ETHN_MAIL_FROM=bookings@example.test`, `ETHN_APP_URL=http://localhost:3000` (match your dev port), `SMTP_HOST=127.0.0.1`, `SMTP_PORT=1025`, and `SMTP_SECURE=false`. Leave SMTP credentials blank and restart Next.js after editing `.env.local`. HTTP origins are allowed only for loopback in development.

Submission, payment review, internal creation, cancellation and refund schedule a booking-scoped best-effort drain after the response. `after()` is not a delivery guarantee. The authenticated backstop retries both customer and owner mail. `vercel.json` schedules `GET /api/cron/ethn-outbox` every five minutes on a supported plan; other hosts need an external scheduler sending `Authorization: Bearer <CRON_SECRET>`. Configure `CRON_SECRET` and verify the schedule after deployment; never put the secret in the URL or logs. For manual use: `curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $CRON_SECRET" "$APP_URL/api/cron/ethn-outbox"`. Confirm scheduler eligibility and pricing for the chosen free host before relying on it.

Each request selects at most five due bookings and claims at most ten rows per booking. This bounds work, not wall-clock time: mail transport may block. Concurrent calls use the worker's locked claims, but delivery is at least once, not exactly once. Future retries wait until due; abandoned claims are eligible after 15 minutes.

## Legacy retirement

The old CK Grounds mail worker, manual drain, and Vercel mail schedule are removed. The old cron and invite API endpoints return `410`; they cannot drain legacy mail or create invite accounts. Legacy outbox rows and invite records were not deleted or migrated. Before rollout, check separately whether the old Supabase `pg_cron` job was installed; it is not managed by Vercel and must be disabled separately if present. The fork-era `supabase/` SQL is archival; do not install the old cron from it.

## Supabase password reset

Password reset is sent by Supabase Auth, not the ETHN booking outbox. The [ETHN reset template](templates/ethn-reset-password.html) is ready for review; repository changes do not publish it. For an approved rollout, back up the existing **Reset password** template in the ETHN Auth project's Email Templates settings, verify Auth Site URL points to the correct ETHN origin, and paste the HTML body with subject **Reset your ETHN Courts password**. Preserve the `{{ .SiteURL }}/reset-password?token_hash={{ .TokenHash }}` link: the ETHN form exchanges the recovery token when the user submits a new password. Verify with an authorized test account in a non-production environment before publishing to a live project. Do not paste real recovery links or tokens into logs or chat.
