# ETHN booking mail

Owner booking alerts appear in the in-app Notifications inbox, including staff-created bookings without a guest email. On-time online payment-proof submissions also queue a separate email for each enabled Owner; mail resolves the Owner’s confirmed Auth email at delivery and checks that owner access is still enabled. The email links to the signed-in booking page and does not include customer contact details, invoice, or proof. Other owner alerts remain in-app only; browser push is not sent. Customer booking emails remain separate.

ETHN sends status updates for booking submission, confirmation, rejection, cancellation and recorded refund. Regular and Open Play submissions use the subject **Booking submitted!** and explain that payment review is required before confirmation. Every booking email has branded HTML and a plain-text alternative. Customer notices include the original booking breakdown: booking date, court ranges, durations, saved slot intervals and hourly rates, line amounts, payment method when available, and the relevant booking, paid, or refund total. Customer name and email appear when the saved booking email matches the recipient. Customer phone stays out of email. Customer updates include the venue address and contact link; confirmations include directions. Online updates use the existing private tracking link or code-and-email lookup. Rejections lead with venue contact and keep tracking available as a secondary link. New staff-created bookings with a guest email include a private tracking link. Older staff-created bookings are not backfilled. No email is sent to staff-booked guests without an address, and archiving never sends an update.

Booking emails embed the ETHN logo. Online payment-proof receipts and customer confirmations with a private tracking link also embed a tracking QR, not a check-in pass. The tracking button still works without images. Where supplied, the booking code works with the customer email. Receipts and confirmations show saved slot counts, durations, per-slot prices (marked approximate when rounding applies), line subtotals, and the booking or paid total. Confirmations include one optional Google Calendar event spanning the first start to the last end. The event description lists actual court ranges so gaps are clear; no private booking link or payment information is sent to Google.

Cancellation does not record a refund. A refund notice reports staff's record of an external refund, not an in-app transfer. Cancellation/refund staff notes, payment references and payment proofs are not included. No historical notifications are backfilled.

Apply `db/ethn/0006_outbox_access.sql` after migrations 0001–0005. Mail is disabled by default. To opt in, set `ETHN_MAIL_ENABLED=1`, `ETHN_MAIL_FROM` to the ETHN sender email, and `ETHN_APP_URL` to the HTTPS ETHN origin. Configure the existing server-only SMTP or Gmail transport separately; do not use legacy branding settings as ETHN sender or origin.

For `npm run dev` with Mailpit, use `ETHN_MAIL_FROM=bookings@example.test`, `ETHN_APP_URL=http://localhost:3000` (match your dev port), `SMTP_HOST=127.0.0.1`, `SMTP_PORT=1025`, and `SMTP_SECURE=false`. Leave SMTP credentials blank and restart Next.js after editing `.env.local`. HTTP origins are allowed only for loopback in development.

Submission, payment review, internal creation, cancellation, recorded refund and the first Open Play added through tracking schedule a booking-scoped best-effort drain after the response. `after()` is not a delivery guarantee. The authenticated backstop retries customer mail. Vercel schedules `GET /api/cron/ethn-outbox` daily (`5 2 * * *`, UTC). Other hosts can use an external scheduler with `Authorization: Bearer <CRON_SECRET>`. Failed mail may wait until the next run or longer if the bounded batch is full. Configure `CRON_SECRET` and verify the schedule after deployment; never put the secret in the URL or logs. For manual use: `curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $CRON_SECRET" "$APP_URL/api/cron/ethn-outbox"`. Confirm scheduler eligibility and pricing for the chosen free host before relying on it.

Each request selects at most five due bookings and claims at most ten rows per booking. This bounds work, not wall-clock time: mail transport may block. Concurrent calls use the worker's locked claims, but delivery is at least once, not exactly once. Future retries wait until due; abandoned claims are eligible after 15 minutes.

## Customer mail in Gmail Spam

When using the Gmail transport, leave `SMTP_HOST` unset and set `ETHN_MAIL_FROM` to the same bare address as `GMAIL_USER`. The app adds the `ETHN Courts` display name separately. Gmail SMTP can rewrite a different From address; copying another mailbox’s header does not authorize it ([Nodemailer Gmail guide](https://nodemailer.com/guides/using-gmail)).

With approval, send an app-generated submission and confirmation to a separate personal Gmail test mailbox. Check Inbox versus Spam, Gmail’s warning if present, and **More → Show original** for the actual sender and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. Share only the verdicts and sender domain, not the raw message, tracking links, customer details, or credentials. Outbox `sent` means SMTP accepted the message, not that Gmail placed it in the inbox. Google recommends clear, expected links and authenticated sending; deleting booking links or changing the display name does not guarantee inbox placement ([Gmail sender guidelines](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/81126)). Keep the booking link unless a controlled comparison points to a specific link problem.

## Legacy retirement

The old CK Grounds mail worker, manual drain, and Vercel mail schedule are removed. The old cron and invite API endpoints return `410`; they cannot drain legacy mail or create invite accounts. Legacy outbox rows and invite records were not deleted or migrated. Before rollout, check separately whether the old Supabase `pg_cron` job was installed; it is not managed by Vercel and must be disabled separately if present. The fork-era `supabase/` SQL is archival; do not install the old cron from it.

## Supabase password reset

Password reset is sent by Supabase Auth, not the ETHN booking outbox. The [ETHN reset template](templates/ethn-reset-password.html) is ready for review; repository changes do not publish it. For an approved rollout, back up the existing **Reset password** template in the ETHN Auth project's Email Templates settings, verify Auth Site URL points to the correct ETHN origin, and paste the HTML body with subject **Reset your ETHN Courts password**. Preserve the `{{ .SiteURL }}/reset-password?token_hash={{ .TokenHash }}` link: the ETHN form exchanges the recovery token when the user submits a new password. Verify with an authorized test account in a non-production environment before publishing to a live project. Do not paste real recovery links or tokens into logs or chat.
